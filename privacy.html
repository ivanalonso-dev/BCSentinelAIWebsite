Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

This page explains what BCSentinel (developed by EMERGE, "we", "us") does and does not do with your information when you visit bcsentinel.ai or write to us.

What we collect

This site has no sign-up, no account, and no contact form that stores data on our servers. The only personal information we receive is what you choose to send us yourself — typically by emailing contact@bcsentinel.ai. That may include your name, email address, organization, and anything else you include in your message.

What we don't collect

We don't run analytics or tracking scripts on this site, and we don't use cookies to identify or follow visitors. See our Cookie Policy for the full detail.

How we use what you send us

We use it only to respond to your inquiry and, if relevant, to follow up about BCSentinel's services. We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Third-party services

This site loads typefaces directly from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). Google's current font-delivery service does not set cookies or log personally identifiable data for this purpose, but your browser does contact Google's servers to fetch the font files, which exposes your IP address to Google as part of that request.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry emails for as long as reasonably needed to respond to you and to maintain a record of institutional contacts, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Your rights

If data protection law in your jurisdiction (for example, the EU/EEA's GDPR) gives you rights over personal data, you can ask us to access, correct, or delete what you've sent us, at any time, by writing to contact@bcsentinel.ai.

Changes to this policy

If we change how this site handles data, we'll update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@bcsentinel.ai
