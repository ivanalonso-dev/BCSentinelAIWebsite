Cookie Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

Short version: bcsentinel.ai does not use cookies for tracking, advertising, or analytics. There is no cookie banner on this site because there is nothing non-essential to ask your consent for.

What this site actually loads

bcsentinel.ai is a static informational site. It has no login, no shopping cart, no saved preferences, and no analytics or advertising scripts — so it sets no cookies of its own.

Google Fonts

The site loads its typefaces directly from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). Per Google's current font-delivery practice, this does not set cookies or log personally identifiable information. Your browser does make a request to Google's servers to fetch the font files, which — independent of any cookie — exposes your IP address to Google as part of that network request, the same way it would for any external resource your browser loads.

If this changes

If we ever add analytics or other tools that use cookies or similar tracking, we'll update this page first and, where required by law, ask for your consent before anything non-essential loads.
